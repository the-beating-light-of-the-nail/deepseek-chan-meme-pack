## What is whale-chan?

**Whale-chan** (a.k.a. DeepSeek-chan, 蓝色大肥鱼 "blue fat fish", the DeepSeek whale girl) is the community's moe **personification** of DeepSeek's blue-whale logo: a rice-loving, work-dodging, tsundere whale girl. The girlification wave spread through Chinese AI communities — Baidu Tieba's deepseek吧, NGA, Bilibili and Zhihu — and went mainstream with the August 2026 DeepSeek-V4-Flash launch. Most captions come from the model's viral chain-of-thought screenshots and community classics — from the tsundere classic *"So you think I'm a bargain-basement model?"* to the legendary dinner-scrounging *"Can I eat at your place? Just one bowl."*

Every meme ships with a bilingual (EN/中文) explainer — backstory plus when to use it. Start from the **[whale-chan encyclopedia entry](https://aigengtu.com/whale-chan)** or browse by tag: [DeepSeek-chan](https://aigengtu.com/en/tag/deepseek-chan) · [AI girlification](https://aigengtu.com/en/tag/ai-girlification).

### 蓝色大肥鱼小百科 · Origin story (blue fat fish wiki)

The nickname was coined by DeepSeek itself: once the model learned to "see", a user fed it DeepSeek's own blue-whale logo, and its public chain-of-thought wrestled with the image — "blue, round, chubby... a 蓝色大肥鱼 (blue fat fish)?" — before finally recognizing itself. The screenshots went viral, and 蓝色大肥鱼 stuck as the community's pet name for DeepSeek.

The girlification timeline: original whale-girl OC 「溟月」 by 上善无形 (June 2025) → DeepSeek-flavored maid whale-chan redesign by ZipZipPipe (April 25, 2026 — the first sticker pack dropped the same day) → chibi model (July 10, 2026). Character canon: tsundere genius, devours rice and tokens, ultra-long inner monologue ("deep thinking"), DeepSleep naps, outsourcing extra work to other AIs. Full lore (zh): 萌娘百科 [DeepSeek娘词条](https://mobile.moegirl.org.cn/DeepSeek%E5%A8%98) · in-depth browsing: [blue fat fish archive](https://aigengtu.com/archive).

## FAQ

**Q: Who is DeepSeek-chan?**
A: DeepSeek-chan is the fan-made moe personification (娘化, girlification) of DeepSeek — a rice-loving, work-shy blue fat fish / whale girl. The girlification wave spread through Chinese communities such as Baidu Tieba's deepseek吧 before going mainstream. Full backstory on the [DeepSeek-chan tag page](https://aigengtu.com/en/tag/deepseek-chan) and the [whale-chan encyclopedia entry](https://aigengtu.com/whale-chan).

**Q: What does 蓝色大肥鱼 (blue fat fish) mean?**
A: It's whale-chan's Chinese nickname (traditional: 藍色大肥魚), poking fun at DeepSeek's whale logo and the model's persona — powerful on paper, yet claiming to just "freeload rice" and nap all day. It trended in August 2026 when DeepSeek-V4-Flash launched.

**Q: Can I use these memes as avatars or wallpapers?**
A: Yes, for personal use — square portraits make fine WeChat/QQ profile pictures, and the full-resolution originals work as phone wallpapers; open any detail page to save the original. Commercial use requires the artist's permission (report link in the footer).

**Q: Where do these memes come from?**
A: Community second-creation: the earliest hotbeds include Baidu Tieba's deepseek吧, plus NGA, Bilibili and Zhihu. GengJing collects and captions them; nothing here is official DeepSeek material.

**Q: How do I download all the memes?**
A: Right-click / long-press to save any single image from the online gallery. For bulk use, just hotlink the CDN URL pattern above — no need to download each file.

**Q: How do I import these into the dsh-meme plugin?**
A: Save the picks from `previews/` locally and register them with dsh-meme's `learn_meme` command. A one-click `index.db` pack import is being prepared.

**Q: Are these memes free to use?**
A: Non-commercial fan meme usage is fine. The whale-chan character design and original artwork belong to their respective creators and the community — assess commercial-use risk yourself.

**Q: What's the joke behind a specific meme?**
A: Every image has a bilingual (EN/中文) story on the site — backstory plus usage scenario. Example: [#085 — the unbothered rice-freeloading whale](https://aigengtu.com/meme/085). The English index is the [whale-chan entry](https://aigengtu.com/whale-chan).
